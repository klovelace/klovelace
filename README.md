# Kraemer Lovelace

**Data Science Leader · Causal Inference & Incrementality · Building Teams That Change How Organizations Decide**

---

I build data science functions that make the rest of the organization smarter — not just better informed. My work sits at the intersection of causal measurement, data engineering, and team leadership: designing the studies that isolate true effects, building the infrastructure that makes results trustworthy, and developing the people who do both.

15 years at USAID leading analytics across an $800M+ portfolio taught me that the hardest part of data science isn't the modeling — it's getting organizations to actually change decisions based on the evidence.

---

### What I work on

**Causal inference & incrementality** — RCTs, geo holdouts, difference-in-differences, propensity score matching, interrupted time series. 30+ studies across high-stakes environments where the counterfactuals were genuinely contested.

**Predictive LTV & marketing measurement** — building the measurement infrastructure that separates true incremental lift from attribution noise, and translating that into budget allocation confidence.

**Data foundations** — metrics frameworks, ETL oversight, self-serve analytics products that give non-technical partners direct access to data and free teams to work on harder problems.

**Team building** — recruiting for analytical rigor, setting strategic direction, and creating the conditions where data scientists generate their best independent thinking.
